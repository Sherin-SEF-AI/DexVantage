The validation report

Every model in DexVantage is checked against an independent reference: a closed form written out again, another library, a published table, brute-force ray casting, Monte Carlo or MuJoCo renders. Each row gives what was compared, against what, the error measured and the tolerance with its reason. The page is generated from the report that dexvantage --validate DIR writes (scripts/build_site.py --validation).
