Systems from the library: hosts, policies, lights, cables and teleop devices

A worked example on the SO-100 sample. The entries, their sources and their checks are in docs/models/library.md; the teleop devices in docs/models/teleop.md.

1. A teleop device from the library

dexvantage --sample so100

Open Analysis ▸ Teleop overlap. Under Teleop devices a second row holds the library picker: choose SO-100 leader arm · Leader arm and press Add from library (one undo step). The new device reads:

  • Leader: the follower's own model, joint to joint. The entry is stated to lead an SO-100 (its leads words match the sample robot's name SO-ARM100 and its model file so_arm100.xml), and LeRobot's SO-100 guide states the leader is built from the follower's parts through the wrist, so same_joints is true.
  • Source: dof: 6 with LeRobot's docs at commit d490e47, read 2026-11-07, and the quote ("Remove the gears of the 6 leader motors"); leader: why the follower's model is used.
  • Below the source: From the library: TheRobotStudio and Hugging Face (LeRobot), SO-100 leader arm. Not stated by the maker: tracking or sample rate, latency, workspace, ...

Add two more from the picker: Quantum Metagloves · Data glove and Koch v1.1 leader arm · Leader arm, then Run. Measured on 2026-11-07 (study defaults, CPU, 10.4 s):

Device Comfortable Teleoperable What the study says
SO-100 leader arm (library) 96.7 % 96.7 % the same as the sample's own leader device: the same joints, joint to joint
Quantum Metagloves — — "its tracking volume is not stated, so its comfortable workspace is not known. Enter a measured or published figure with its source; DexVantage does not guess one."
Koch v1.1 leader arm — — "the library entry "Koch v1.1 leader arm" states the leader's figures but no kinematics, and it is not stated to lead this follower; add the leader arm as a robot (Robot ▸ Import) and choose it as the leader."

The glove's figures (120 Hz sample rate, ≤ 7.5 ms signal latency, 138 g) are in its source with MANUS's page and the date read; the reach model does not use them. To give the glove a region, measure the volume its wrist tracker covers and enter it under Volume with its source.

2. A policy variant

Tasks ▸ Policies: the list now includes π0.5, GR00T N1.5 3B, RDT-1B, OpenVLA-OFT (LIBERO) and OpenVLA-OFT+ (ALOHA), the SmolVLA LIBERO and Meta-World fine-tunes, Diffusion Policy (CNN, Push-T), ACT (LeRobot defaults), HPT-Base and Octo-Small. Each figure shows its source; a figure no source states shows "—". For example OpenVLA-OFT+ (ALOHA): three 224 × 224 images, the centre 90 % crop of the area (0.9487 of each side), chunk 25 executed open-loop, 321 ms per chunk on an NVIDIA A100, a 25 Hz controller — all from Kim, Finn and Liang (2025) and the official code. π0.5 states no inference time: the action-chunk study says so rather than assuming one.

3. A host and a cable

Analysis ▸ Bandwidth, hosts and power: Basler USB 3.0 Card PCIe, Fresco FL1100, 4 HC, x4, 4 Ports (5 Gbit/s) is a USB 3.2 Gen 1 controller card; its entry carries Basler's "Up to 380 MBps per port reliably supported" (3 040 Mbit/s) and "Up to 1.52 GBps total" (12 160 Mbit/s). Enter the per-port figure as the controller's practical rate (its source is in the entry's note) to budget against it. The Jetson AGX Thor Developer Kit lists two USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-A ports (10 Gbit/s each), two USB-C ports whose generation NVIDIA does not state, and a QSFP28 (4 × 25 GbE) for Holoscan Sensor Bridge cameras.

Analysis ▸ Cable flex across joints: Basler Cable USB 3.0, Micro B sl/A, P states "Minimum Bending Radius 62 mm, fixed installation" and "Suitable for Robotics Applications No"; across a moving joint the study uses the fixed radius and says the maker states no moving figure. Stereolabs GMSL2 Fakra Cable F-F states 33 mm static and 66 mm "loaded" (taken as the moving radius) and 10 000 flex cycles.

4. Check the entries

dexvantage --validate out/validation

The library model's systems checks (library.teleop_figures, library.policy_token_grid, library.stated_totals) pass with 0 problems, 0 tokens and 9.0 × 10⁻⁴ (the HaptX glove's "~ 1 lb" entered as 454 g). dexvantage --self-test has the line "library teleop".
