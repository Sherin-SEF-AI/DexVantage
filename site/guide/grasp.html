Grasp self-occlusion: a worked example on the UR5e sample

At the moment the fingers close, can the cameras see the fingertips and the object? A policy learns contact only from what its cameras show. The Grasp self-occlusion page answers this for a whole grasp set: it solves the arm for every grasp, asks every camera which parts of each fingertip's contact patch it sees, and reports the share of grasps where your requirement holds, the Contact Visibility Score, with its interval. The model, its definitions, its validation and the measured timings are in Grasp self-occlusion.

1. Make a grasp set

  1. File ▸ Open sample ▸ UR5e and Robotiq 2F-85 with a wrist D405 and an overhead D435. The rig is "Wrist D405" on the flange and "Overhead D435" on the cage above a 0.50 × 0.40 × 0.30 m tabletop task volume (sample placements).
  2. The sample already carries the set "Tabletop grasps (sample)": the same 200 grasps as below, so you can go straight to step 2. To make it yourself, open the Tasks page, delete that set, and under the sampling form tick Top-down pinch, Side grasp and Angled, leave Count at 200 and Seed at 0, and press Apply. The set "Sampled: top-down pinch, side grasp, angled (200)" appears in the table and as gripper glyphs in the 3D view. Grasp sets are described in Grasp sets: a worked example on the UR5e and SO-100 samples; you can also import a CSV or JSON of gripper poses or take a trajectory.

2. Run the study

  1. Analysis ▸ Grasp self-occlusion opens the page (Grasp study workspace). The Grasp set box shows the set, Cameras reads "All cameras in use", the requirement "Every fingertip", IK "Nearest to home", and the numbers below are labelled "default".
  2. Press Run. On a recent GPU the 200 grasps take about 25 s: inverse kinematics, the cube maps at contact and at 2.5 and 5 cm before it, "what to move" and the gallery. The note under the page says how fast it went and on which renderer.

The headline reads

100.0 [97.2–100] % of reachable grasps · well seen

with "100 % of the 133 reachable grasps have every fingertip at least 50 % visible in at least one camera. … Every resample gives the same score, so the bootstrap interval is a point; the figure shows Wilson's 95 % score interval instead, 97.2 % to 100.0 %." The 100 % is real, not a weak test: the wrist D405 sees both fingertips in every grasp; the overhead D435 alone sees them in only 35 % (the camera table). Hover over the figure for the counts, the interval's method and the thresholds (good from 90 %, warning from 70 %, serious from 40 %: DexVantage defaults, not standards).

Under the settings: "133 of 200 grasps reachable without a collision; unreachable: 64 obstacle collision, 2 out of reach, 1 no convergence; most obstacle contacts: wrist_1_link × Table (sample) (49), …". The sampler puts the grasp centre 30 mm above the table; for a side grasp that low, the UR5e's wrist hits the table. That is about the task, not the cameras: unreachable grasps are left out of the score (the export also gives the share of all grasps).

3. Read the camera table

Camera Fingertips visible Both ≥ threshold Pre-contact Seen Outside image Behind own finger Hidden by scene
Wrist D405 95 %, 95 % 100 % 95 % 95 % 0 % 0 % 5 %
Overhead D435 42 %, 42 % 35 % 33 % 42 % 0 % 25 % 33 %
  • The wrist camera looks down the gap between the fingers and sees both pads in every grasp: it alone carries the score.
  • The overhead camera sees 42 % of the contact points. A quarter are on the far side of their own finger (the pad's face is turned away from it), and a third are hidden by the scene, mostly by the gripper itself above the pads.
  • "Pre-contact" is the weaker fingertip's visible share on the last 5 cm of the approach.

4. One camera at a time

What if the wrist camera is not there (a different gripper, or a dataset recorded without it)? Choose Cameras ▸ Overhead D435 (one undo step) and press Run again:

34.6 [26–43] % of reachable grasps · blind at contact

with "35 % of the 133 reachable grasps have every fingertip at least 50 % visible in at least one camera." Wilson's interval is 27.0 to 43.0 %. Now:

  • Worst grasps shows six renders from the D435, all top-down grasps with score 0: seen from above, a top-down grasp puts the gripper between the camera and the pads. Contact points are drawn green (visible), red (hidden by the scene), orange (behind their own finger) and grey (outside the image). Click a render, or a row of the grasp table, to put the robot in that grasp's pose; the Camera view then shows it live.
  • The 3D view shows every grasp centre coloured by its score: blue where the requirement holds, orange where it does not (darker further from it), grey where the arm cannot reach. The Camera view draws the same centres as crosses.
  • What to move ranks the D435 moved by 2 cm along each world axis or turned by 5° (defaults). The best is "move Overhead D435 −2 cm along x of world" at +2 points, but its interval reaches below zero, and the advice says it may be noise: small moves of this camera do not change what a top-down grasp hides. A camera at the side of the table, looking at the pinch in profile, would see both fingertips; add one (Sensors ▸ Add camera) and run again.
  • Preview draws where the best move would put the camera; Apply makes it (one undo step) and marks the result for a new run.

Set Cameras back to "All cameras in use" (or undo) before going on.

5. Change the requirement

  • Fingertip visible from (default 50 %) is the share of a patch that must be seen; Cameras needed (default 1) how many cameras must see it. Changing them, the requirement (any fingertip, the object region, or both) or the IK rule needs a new Run; changing Good from, Warning from or Serious from only re-reads the status.
  • Other settings live in project.analysis.studies['grasp_visibility'] (see the model page): the pre-contact distance, the lift of the contact location in front of the pad (2 mm), the cube-map size, the q-bucket size, the bootstrap resamples and seed, the step of "what to move".

6. Export and batch

Export… writes the result with provenance: every grasp (id, kind, reachable and why, score, joint vector, each fingertip's visible share per camera, pre-contact), the cameras, the moves and the gallery.

Without the window, on a saved project that has a grasp set:

dexvantage cell.dexv --grasp grasp.json

writes the same JSON. On a project without a grasp set the command stops with "There is no grasp set to study: make one on the Tasks page …" and exit code 2. Large sets (above 2,000 grasps) run in the background in the window and show partial results; 2,000 grasps on this sample took 112 s on an RTX 5080 and 131 s on the integrated Radeon of a Ryzen 9 9900X (the model page has the breakdown).

Known limits

Visibility is judged per cube-map texel, so a point at an edge may go either way; the fingers stay at their home opening; the contact patch comes from the collision geometry; IK is a local method and obstacles are checked at contact only; "what to move" moves one camera along one axis at a time on a subset of 250 grasps. The full list is on the model page.
