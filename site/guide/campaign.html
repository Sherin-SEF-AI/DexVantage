Planning a data-collection campaign: a worked example on the ALOHA 2 sample

This plans the demonstrations for the ALOHA 2 sample's handover under two lighting presets, three operators, enough demonstrations per preset to tell a 50 % success rate from 70 %, and a budget of 4 TB, ten days and an 8-hour upload window on a 1 Gbit/s link. The model is in docs/models/campaign.md. Figures below are from a run on 2026-11-07.

1. The spec

Data ▸ Campaign shows the settings as fields (each change one undo step, "(default)" while a figure is DexVantage's). The levels of the conditions (lighting presets, object sets, variants, teleop devices, tasks) live in a spec file, read with Open spec… or given to batch mode:

{"schema": "dexvantage.campaign_spec/1", "name": "ALOHA handover, two lighting presets",
 "operators": ["Ana", "Ben", "Chen"],
 "lighting": [{"name": "Bright"}, {"name": "Dim"}],
 "demos": {"mode": "power", "p_base": 0.5, "p_target": 0.7},
 "episode_s": 30, "start_date": "2026-11-16",
 "budget": {"disk_gb": 4000, "days": 10, "upload_mbps": 1000, "upload_hours_per_day": 8}}

The sample's two trajectories (left and right arm over the same 4 s) are one bimanual task, "Handover"; its 4 s is the scripted motion, so episode_s sets 30 s for a person teleoperating. The sample has no lights in the project, so the presets are names only and the checklist says "room light as it is"; add the lights (Workspace ▸ Lights) and name them in each preset, {"name": "Dim", "lights": {"Key panel": 0.3}}, to have the checklist say which to switch.

2. Plan

dexvantage --sample aloha --campaign spec.json

prints Campaign: 216 episodes over 2 days, 1,304.0 GB (within budget) and writes spec.plan.json (with provenance), spec.plan.csv, spec.plan.ics and spec.plan.html. On the page the headline reads 216 "episodes over 2 days, 1,304.0 GB", status within budget: storage 33 % of the disk, days 20 %, the busiest day's upload 30 % of the window (2.4 h).

  • Demonstrations. The power analysis (two-sided, α 0.05, 80 % power, with the continuity correction: defaults) asks for 103 per condition. Three operators recording each condition in two equal blocks makes that 108 (the note says so): 36 per operator per condition, blocks of 18.
  • Design. "Williams balanced Latin square of order 2 (2 rows): 6 sequences (3 operators × 2 rounds) … at every position equally often; and after every other condition equally often (carry-over balanced)." With seed 0, Ana records Dim then Bright, then Bright then Dim; Ben Dim, Bright twice; Chen Bright, Dim twice: three sequences start with each preset.
  • Storage. The bandwidth study's rates of the four D405 streams, 1 563 Mbit/s raw, plus its 3 % container overhead (a default): 6.04 GB per 30 s episode. Recording to a LeRobot dataset with a ratio you measured on one episode ("format": "lerobot", "compression_ratio": {"value": 0.05, "source": "episode 0 of the pilot"}) scales it; without a ratio the page says the raw size is used.
  • Sessions. Five sessions in 4.5 h on the rig: day 1 (2026-11-16) Ana 09:10–10:37 (72 episodes; her sequences repeat Bright at the round boundary, so one changeover fewer lets them fit in the 90-minute limit), Ben, Chen, Ben; day 2 Chen 09:10–09:56. The 10-minute daily calibration check moves the first session to 09:10.

3. The checklist and the calendar

Click a session for its checklist. The first one starts with the calibration check ("capture 5 poses of the hand-eye plan … with DexVantage's default board (ChArUco 9 × 7, 30 mm squares, a default) on cam_left_wrist … within 0.5 mm and 0.05° (defaults)"), then the rig variant, the lighting preset of each condition, the teleop devices (both leader arms), each camera's exposure ("not set: run Analysis ▸ Motion blur and Apply its exposure" until you do; after the Motion blur page's Apply the line reads the exposure and where it came from), the disk to free (434.68 GB) and the blocks. spec.plan.ics opens in any calendar program: one event per session, the checklist in its description, at local time. spec.plan.html prints a day per page with tick boxes.

4. Change and apply

Lower the disk budget to 1 000 GB: the headline turns over budget and the advice reads "Storage 1,304.0 GB against 1,000 GB: record to a compressed format (enter its measured ratio), lower a frame rate on the Bandwidth page, or add disk." Put it back, then Apply: the plan goes into the project (one undo step; the notes say "The project holds an applied plan (this one)"). Save spec… writes the spec for --campaign in CI.

From Python:

import dexvantage as dv
rig = dv.sample('aloha')
res = dv.campaign.plan(rig, 'spec.json')
res.headline['text']          # 'Campaign episodes recorded: 216 (within budget)'
dv.campaign.export(res, 'out/plan')       # out/plan.csv, .ics, .html
dv.campaign.apply(rig, res)               # the plan into the project
