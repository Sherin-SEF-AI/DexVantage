Prices and the rig cost

The library carries cited list prices (docs/models/library.md#prices): one unit's price as the maker's own store or official price list stated it on the date read, with the page's URL. The rig inventory adds them up as rig.cost. Nothing is estimated and no currency is converted.

Worked example: the UR5e sample

import dexvantage as dv

rig = dv.sample('ur5e')                      # a Wrist D405 and an Overhead D435
rig.metrics(['rig.cost'])['rig.cost']
# {'value': 700.0, 'unit': 'USD', 'status': 'ok', ...}

dv.price('intel-realsense-d405')
# {'amount': 325, 'currency': 'USD', 'unit': 'each', 'kind': 'maker_store',
#  'source': 'https://store.realsenseai.com/buy-realsense-depth-camera-d405.html', 'read': '2026-11-07', ...}

dv.prices('depth').to_frame()                # every depth camera's price, source and date

From the command line the same figure is in the rig inventory's JSON:

python -m dexvantage --sample ur5e --rig-inventory out/rig.json     # cost: {"value": 700.0, "unit": "USD", ...}

When a part has no price: the dexhand sample

The dexhand sample's palm camera (OmniVision OVM6948) has no price on a maker's page, so the cost is a lower bound:

dv.sample('dexhand').metrics(['rig.cost'])['rig.cost']
# {'value': 574.0, 'bound': 'at_least', 'status': 'ok',
#  'reason': 'At least 574.00 USD: a price is not stated for 1 of the 3 parts (Palm micro camera ...), ...'}

A requirement rig.cost <= 500 USD fails on it (at least 574 cannot be at most 500); rig.cost <= 1000 USD stays "not evaluated" until the palm camera has a price. To give it one, add a custom library entry (a JSON file in the user library, docs/models/library.md, Custom devices) with a price block naming your quote (the figures below are placeholders for yours, not a price):

"price": {"amount": 120, "currency": "USD", "unit": "each", "kind": "reseller",
          "source": "https://your-distributor.example/quote-123", "read": "2026-11-07", "what": "quote for 10 pcs"}

In the optimiser

rig.cost is one of the optimiser's default objectives. It is used only when every device the search can choose states a price; otherwise it is dropped with a sentence naming the devices without one. On the UR5e and ALOHA samples (RealSense cameras only) it is used.

Prices in another currency

Every built-in price is in US dollars. A custom entry in another currency gives the rig inventory a total in that currency, but rig.cost (a USD figure) reads "not stated" for it, and a rig mixing currencies is refused: no exchange rate is assumed.

Known limits: list prices without tax, shipping or discounts, on the date read; volume prices (NVIDIA modules, 1000+) are marked min_quantity; only the enabled sensors are counted; prices go stale, and the validation check fails a price read more than two years ago.
