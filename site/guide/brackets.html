Brackets: a worked example on the UR5e sample

This walks through printable brackets for the two cameras of the UR5e + Robotiq 2F-85 sample: an adapter on the robot's ISO 9409-1 flange for the wrist D405, and a 2020 T-slot clamp for the overhead D435. The model behind it is in docs/models/brackets.md. Figures below are from a run on 2026-11-06 at the default settings.

1. Open the sample and run

dexvantage --sample ur5e

Open Analysis ▸ Brackets and press Run. Neither sensor has a bracket type yet, so the headline reads "—" with the status word check and the line "No sensor has a bracket type yet: Apply sets the suggested ones. Suggested: Wrist D405: wrist-flange adapter (ISO 9409-1); Overhead D435: camera clamp, 2020 T-slot."

The suggestions come from the mounts: the D405 sits on wrist_3_link, whose fixed child is the gripper's coupling, and the UR5e's flange is ISO 9409-1-50-4-M6 by UR's datasheet; the D435 is on the cage, a static frame.

Preview shows the suggestions with their reasons; Apply sets both types in one undo step (Edit ▸ Undo takes them back) and runs the study again (about 17 s: most of it is the clearance check over the joint range).

2. Read the result

The headline now reads 0/2 ready, status check. Both brackets are closed, printable solids; both carry a warning that is about the camera, not the bracket:

Wrist D405 (flange adapter) Overhead D435 (2020 clamp)
Size 63 × 95 × 24 mm 121 × 29 × 12 mm
Mass (solid PETG, an estimate) 38 g 32 g
Mesh closed, one shell, V − E + F = −12 (7 through holes) closed, one shell, V − E + F = −2
Camera face bottom, 1 × 1/4-20 (Intel Table 4-22: 9 mm from the left imager) back, 2 × M3, 45 mm apart (Intel Figure 10-9)
Clearance forearm_link 2.0 mm inside in 1 of 48 sampled states (2 %) no link comes near
Image covered 0.0 % 0.0 %

Select a row to see its holes and figures. For the adapter: four Ø6.4 mm bolt holes on the Ø50 circle at 45°, 135°, 225° and 315° and a Ø6.1 mm dowel hole on +x, all marked cited (the tooltip gives UR's datasheet and the ISO preview); a Ø31.4 mm centring spigot 3 mm high; the gripper's footprint (77 mm) kept free above the disc. The flags say what to check before printing:

  • "The 1 × 1/4-20 on the bottom face: the maker's drawing does not dimension where they sit on the face, so they are drawn at its middle" — Intel gives the hole's distance from the left imager, not its position front to back.
  • "The depth of the flange's Ø31.5 centring recess is not stated: the adapter's spigot is 3 mm high (a default)".

The notes give the assembly: the bolts get 8 mm longer, UR's limit on bolt engagement is quoted, the gripper sits 8 mm further out (add it to the tool centre point), a Ø6 mm dowel sets the camera's angle about the flange axis, and the print orientation (on its side, about 10 cm² of overhang to support).

The clearance line means: in one of the 48 collision-free joint states sampled within the UR5e's limits, the forearm folds back far enough to touch the camera. That is a warning, not a fault: avoid that arm pose, or move the camera. The notes say 83 of 144 drawn states were dropped because the robot ran into itself.

3. Change it and export

  • In the 3D view the brackets are drawn grey on the robot, with the camera body's box outlined in the status colour; they follow the arm as you move the joints. The Camera view outlines them too (useful to see whether the overhead camera sees the wrist bracket).
  • Choose the sensor, then its Bracket type, flange pattern, side, standoff, tilt and wall: each change is one undo step and is kept in the project (the type on the sensor's mount, the options in the study settings). Run again.
  • Export STL… writes the selected bracket at full resolution (0.5 mm grid) as Wrist_D405.stl with Wrist_D405.stl.json beside it: the STL's SHA-256, the dimensions, holes and their sources, flags, notes, the defaults used and the provenance block. Meshing at full resolution takes about a second, so the button builds and writes on a worker thread (the status bar shows it) and says when the files are written; a folder that does not exist is refused at once. Export… writes the whole result as JSON.

4. Batch

dexvantage --sample ur5e --brackets brackets.json

writes the same result (with provenance) for the sensors that have a bracket type. Set the types first in the project (or in the page) and save it; --brackets on a project file uses its types and options.

What is not checked

Strength, stiffness and heat are not modelled. The camera body is a box of the maker's stated size with the optics at the front face (an assumption). Where the T-slot clamp sits on the profile and the hand's surface under a palm mount are not in the project; the base is placed from the camera (side, standoff, tilt). See the model's Known limits.
