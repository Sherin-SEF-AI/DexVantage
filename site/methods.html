The models behind DexVantage

One document per model: what it computes and from which inputs, its equations as the code evaluates them, the references it follows, the validation checks that compare it with an independent reference and the agreement they measured, and its known limits. Models and methods gives the overview of all of them; the validation report gives every check with its error and tolerance.
