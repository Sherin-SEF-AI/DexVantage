Synthetic LeRobot episodes with ground truth

Module: dexvantage/core/sim/episodes.py (settings_of, plan, generate, batch_generate). Command: Simulate ▸ Generate synthetic episodes… (dexvantage/ui/dialogs/synth_episodes.py, a window job with progress). Batch: --synth-episodes OUT_DIR [--synth-settings JSON]. Check: groundtruth.synthetic_roundtrip (dexvantage/validation/checks_synth.py, model groundtruth). Tests: tests/test_synth_episodes.py. Throughput: scripts/measure_synth.py. Spec §9.1, last bullet: "Generate synthetic episodes in LeRobot format (v2.1 and v3.0) with ground truth, so the auditor and the training pipelines can be tested."

The generator moves the project's robot, renders every camera through its own lens model with the MuJoCo ground-truth renderer (core/sim/render.py: pinhole, Brown-Conrady and the fisheye models through the distortion remap), writes the episodes with the LeRobot writer (core/datasets/lerobot.py, v2.1 or v3.0) and writes the ground truth beside them as dexvantage_truth.json (Dataset.truth() returns it). Faults are injected at seeded, known places. Nothing in the dataset files marks a fault: the auditor (§8.2) has to find them from the data and can then be scored against the sidecar.

Motion

Source When How
Trajectories motion auto and the project has any, or trajectories episode e plays trajectory e mod n of the first trajectory's robot (core/robot/trajectory.py: linear in the joints between samples; joints it does not name at home), time-scaled to the episode when frames is set, else at its own speed
Grasp approaches auto with no trajectory, or grasps home → pre-grasp (pre_grasp_m = 80 mm back along the approach) → grasp → hold → pre-grasp. Each waypoint by IK (ik.solve_batch, 4 seeds, self-collision off as in the motion-blur study's approaches, the pre-grasp nearest the grasp). Grasps drawn without replacement from the first grasp set with the seed

Minimum-jerk timing between approach waypoints:

q(t) = q_k + (q_{k+1} − q_k) · s(τ),   s(τ) = 10τ³ − 15τ⁴ + 6τ⁵,   τ = (t − T_k) / (T_{k+1} − T_k)
T_{k+1} − T_k = max(0.3 s, max_j |Δq_j| / v_max) · u,   v_max = 0.8 rad/s,  u ~ U(0.85, 1.15) (seeded)

so the joints stand still at every waypoint (tested: |q̇| < 10⁻³ rad/s there). Each episode adds a seeded constant joint offset δ ~ N(0, 0.02 rad) (gripper joints left out) and is clipped to the limits. Rows: t_i = i / fps, observation.state = q(t_i), action = q(t_i + L / fps) with L = action_lead_rows = 1 (the commanded next state; the last rows hold). 0.3 s, 0.8 rad/s, 80 mm, 0.02 rad and L = 1 are DexVantage defaults, not measurements.

Cameras and timelines

One camera per enabled camera or depth sensor: the scene's primary imager (the RGB imager of a depth camera) at its stream size, scaled to at most max_width = 640 px wide with even sides (yuv420p needs them), or to size [w, h]. Frame rate: the stream's (the bandwidth study's stream choice, as motion_blur.imaging reads it), else default_fps = 30 fps (a default, said in the sidecar's fps_source). The dataset's fps is the largest camera rate as a whole number (LeRobot stores one). Key: observation.images.<sensor name>, lower case, the parenthesised part dropped.

Camera c has slots k = 0 … M − 1, M = ⌊D · fps_c⌋ + 1 over the rows' span D, at nominal times t_k = k / fps_c. Per slot the generator keeps

stamp      s_k = t_k + n_k                       (n_k = 0 unless the camera has jitter)
content    c_k = t_k + Δ + ρ · t_k               (Δ: desync offset, ρ: drift rate; 0 unless injected)

The stored timestamp is s_k; the image shows the robot at q(c_k). Every image gets seeded Gaussian noise of noise_dn = 1 grey level (a default, not a sensor model), so two real frames are never bit-identical while a duplicate is.

Faults

Each fault is switchable and has its own generator seeded with (seed, episode, fault), so turning one off leaves the others where they were (tested). episodes (default every episode) and camera (default: a seeded choice among the cameras frame faults have used least) can be set per fault. Frame faults go to interior slots (two from each end) that no other frame fault uses, a duplicate's previous frame included; when an episode is too short, fewer are placed and the notes say so. Every figure is a DexVantage default chosen so the fault shows in a short episode.

Fault Default In the data
drop 2 per episode slots not stored: a stamp gap of two periods
duplicate 2 the stored frame is the previous stored frame's pixels exactly, at its own stamp; placed where the joints move fastest
desync Δ = 0.05 s c_k = t_k + Δ for one camera
drift ρ = 5000 ppm c_k = t_k + ρ t_k (exaggerated so it shows within seconds)
jitter σ = 3 ms n_k ~ N(0, σ), clipped to ±0.2 period (stamps stay ordered; a regular gap stays < 1.4 periods and a drop gap > 1.6)
exposure_change gain 1.4 over 25 % of the frames a contiguous run of frames times the gain, clipped at 255
blur 2 frames, 9 subframes, E = 1 / fps the mean of 9 renders at c_k + E (j/8 − ½), j = 0 … 8 (temporal supersampling); placed where the joints move fastest. Other frames are one instant
gripper_hidden 3 consecutive frames in the wrist camera (the first camera on a link of the moving robot), a flat dark rectangle painted over the gripper's pixels grown by 5 % of the width
mount_slip from episode ⌊n/2⌋, ΔT = (3 mm, 0, 0), (0, 1.5°, 0) one camera (default the first static one) renders through T_body_sensor · ΔT; ΔT in the sensor body, its rotation a rotation vector in degrees (the §8.5 mount-tolerance defaults)
usb_reset 0.25 s a contiguous run of round(0.25 · fps) slots not stored (at most a fifth of the episode)

The ground-truth sidecar

dexvantage_truth.json, schema dexvantage.truth/1, in the dataset root:

  • project_digest, generator (every setting, the seed, the defaults used, the codec, the renderer, the robot's joint names and gripper links, the conventions in words, the notes) and a provenance block;
  • cameras {key: sensor_uid, imager, fps, fps_source, wrist, intrinsics {model, width, height, K, dist} of the stored images, extrinsics {frame: the mount's link or world, pose: the optical frame (z forward, x right, y down) in it, position and quaternion (x, y, z, w)}: the true mount};
  • episodes {"e": faults [{kind, camera, frames, slots, and offset_s | drift_ppm | jitter_s | gain | exposure_s | delta | gap_s}], extrinsics {key: the mount actually used}, visibility {key: gripper_visible per stored frame, gripper_px, min_px}, timing {key: slot, stamp_s, content_time_s per stored frame}, rows, motion}.

frames are indices into the camera's stored frames (CameraStream.frames); for drop and usb_reset they are the stored frames right after each gap and slots lists the missing slots. The gripper counts as visible when at least gripper_min_frac = 0.2 % of the image (a default) is the gripper's links (the gripper frame's body and every body below it) in the rendered segmentation, after the occluder.

Validation

groundtruth.synthetic_roundtrip generates two SO-100 episodes (16 rows, 64 × 48 px, every fault on, seed 3) at v2.1 and v3.0, reads them back through open_dataset and compares them with the generator's own arrays and the sidecar it returned. The LeRobot reader itself is checked independently (pyarrow and the ffmpeg command-line tools) by formats.lerobot_roundtrip. Counted as mismatches, tolerance 0:

Quantity Tolerance and why Measured 2026-11-07
state, action, time columns bit-exact after the float32 cast LeRobot stores (the writer casts once) exact
frame counts per camera (reader, generator, sidecar timing) equal equal
camera stamps half a 1/90000 s tick (irregular cameras are stored at that time base) within
decoded frame against its generated frame ≥ 30 dB PSNR, and not closer to a neighbour that differs by more than the codec error (3 dB margin) worst 33.7 dB (v2.1 and v3.0, AV1)
duplicate against the frame it repeats, both decoded ≥ 30 dB: LeRobot's g = 2 makes every other frame a keyframe, so a repeated picture is coded again farthest pair 36.2 dB (v3.0), 39.8 dB (v2.1)
gaps (≥ 1.5 periods) exactly before the frames the sidecar's drop and USB-reset faults list exact
sidecar the read-back document equals the returned one; one visibility entry per stored frame; fault frames in range equal

Result: 0 mismatches over 54 frames per version, 1.3 s on the RTX 5080. The tests also check the faults in the pixels against fresh renders (noise off): a duplicate is bit-identical to its previous frame; a blurred frame has a lower Laplacian variance than the sharp render at its content time; an exposure frame equals the sharp render times the gain within 1 grey level; the occluder leaves no gripper pixel; the slipped camera's image differs from the unslipped render; the desynced camera's content time is stamp + Δ (+ ρ t_k when it also drifts). The same seed gives the same truth and bit-identical frames.

This check confirms that the files carry exactly the faults the generator injected. Detecting them from the data alone is the auditor's job and its own checks.

Throughput

scripts/measure_synth.py, every fault on, default settings unless stated (4 episodes, each motion at its own length), 2026-11-07, Ryzen 9 9900X. "Render" is the wall time of the render calls (MuJoCo through EGL in the worker process, the transfer and the lens remap); "other" is mostly AV1 encoding (libsvtav1, LeRobot's options), then the per-frame noise and Parquet.

Known limits

Images are MuJoCo's OpenGL shading: no auto-exposure dynamics, rolling shutter or sensor noise model; the 1-grey-level noise only keeps real frames distinct. Blur averages 8-bit images, not linear radiance. The gripper occluder is painted in the image (no body, no shadow, no depth). The motion is kinematic: no dynamics and no tracking error besides the action lead; grasp approaches are not checked against obstacles and do not close the gripper. One robot moves and its joints are the state vector; other robots stay at home. One imager per sensor is recorded. Drift is a constant rate and jitter Gaussian; faults are placed independently and several on one camera combine (desync, drift and jitter add; an exposure run can include a blurred frame). The drift default (5000 ppm) is far above a real oscillator's so that it shows within one episode.
