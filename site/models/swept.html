Swept volume of a trajectory

core/robot/swept.py, drawn by the Tasks page (Swept volume, spec §13 "the ghosted swept volume of a trajectory"). A picture of where the arm goes along a joint trajectory, not a measurement: nothing is computed from it (the cable study tests snags at its own sampled poses, core/cables.py; the grasp study tests collisions at each grasp, core/studies/grasp_visibility.py).

The model

  • Meshes. The robot's visual triangles (RobotModel.local_triangles, each in its own link's frame) are simplified once per compiled model by vertex clustering (core/scenegeom.decimate, Rossignac and Borrel 1993), link by link in the link's own frame, with a cell of 10 mm (GHOST_CELL_M, a default). On the samples: UR5e 193 113 triangles, SO-100 60 682, the dexhand sample 179 519 before; the decimation takes about 0.36, 0.08 and 0.32 s (measured 2026-11-07, Ryzen 9 9900X), so the page runs it as a background job the first time and caches it per model.
  • Poses. GHOST_POSES (8, a default; at most 64) times evenly spaced over the trajectory's time range, its first and last sample included; at each the trajectory is mapped onto the model's joints (trajectory.on_model: joints it does not name at home, values clipped to the limits) and each link's simplified triangles are carried by its forward-kinematics transform: p_world = R_link(q(t)) p_link + t_link(q(t)).
  • Drawing. One translucent TriDraw in the trajectory colour, alpha rising linearly from 0.10 at the first time to 0.35 at the last (fainter early, stronger late, so the direction of motion reads).

Validation

No validation check: the ghosts are a display, and the only computation is forward kinematics, which the kinematics checks hold against closed-form DH and finite differences. tests/test_spec_review_p367.py checks that each ghost is its link's simplified mesh carried by fk_array at that time (10⁻⁵ m, float32 storage) and lies within a cell of the full meshes' bounds at that pose.

Known limits

  • A set of poses, not the continuous swept volume: what the arm passes through between two ghosts is not drawn.
  • The simplified meshes are coarser than the cell near thin parts (a clamp, a finger, a cable) and can lose them.
  • The Camera view does not show the ghosts.
