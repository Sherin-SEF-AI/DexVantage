Sparse voxels with adaptive refinement

Module: dexvantage/core/voxels.py. Tests: tests/test_voxels.py. Spec §7.1.

The grid

voxels.SparseGrid.from_project(project, voxel_mm=None, refine_mm=None, margin_m=0.02) covers each task volume (an axis-aligned box, project.workspace.volumes) with cubic base voxels of edge voxel_mm (default project.analysis.voxel_mm, 5 mm by default): ceil(edge / voxel) per axis, as core.jobs.voxel_count counts them, the grid centred on the volume so it overhangs each face by less than half a voxel.

A base voxel is refined, split into k³ voxels with k = ceil(voxel / refine) so that the refined edge voxel / k is at most refine_mm (default project.analysis.refine_mm, 2 mm by default: 5 mm / 3 = 1.67 mm), when

  • its volume is a refinement region (Volume.refine), or
  • it overlaps a task object's bounding box grown by margin_m (20 mm, a default): the object and its contact zone.

The grid gives .centers (N, 3) float32 world metres, .size (N,) float32 edge metres, .volume_index (N,) int32 into .volumes, .refined (N,) bool, .count, .volume_m3 and .chunks(n), which yields Chunk(start, stop, centers, size, volume_index) views of at most n voxels so a study's per-voxel temporaries stay bounded. The voxels' volumes add up to the base grid's volume exactly (refinement splits, it never adds).

The limit and its message

voxels.estimate(project, voxel_mm=None, refine_mm=None) counts the voxels before anything is built (a byte per base cell for the refinement mask) and returns count, dense_count (a dense grid at the refined size over the same volumes), feasible (count ≤ core.jobs.DENSE_LIMIT, 5 × 10⁷ voxels) and a plain-English message. SparseGrid.from_project refuses a grid over the limit with core.jobs.StudyError(message). Spec §7.1's example reads, for one 1.5 × 1.5 × 1.2 m volume at 1 mm:

A dense 1 mm grid over 1.5 × 1.5 × 1.2 m is 2.7 × 10⁹ voxels, which is over the limit of 5 × 10⁷ and is not built: use a coarser voxel size and keep the fine size to refinement regions around the objects or the contact zone.

At the default 5 mm the same volume is 2.2 × 10⁷ voxels and fits; the message then says so, and that a dense 1.7 mm grid would be 5.8 × 10⁸ voxels, over the limit. voxels.dense_message(size_m, voxel_m) writes the sentence for any box.

Sizes on the samples: the UR5e tabletop volume (0.50 × 0.40 × 0.30 m) is 480,000 voxels at 5 mm; the SO-100's (0.30 × 0.20 × 0.12 m) is 57,600.

Known limits

  • One level of refinement (base → k³), not a full octree.
  • The refined zone around an object is its bounding box grown by the margin, not its shape.
  • Volumes are axis-aligned boxes; where two volumes overlap, the shared space has voxels for each (each volume's statistics stay whole), so a sum over all voxels counts it twice.
  • The 5 × 10⁷ limit (core.jobs.DENSE_LIMIT) is a memory bound of this build (about 1 GB for the centres, sizes, volume indices and refinement flags at 21 bytes per voxel), not a property of the method.
